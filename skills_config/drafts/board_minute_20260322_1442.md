## Agenda Items

### 5.1 Funding for Childcare Options

A discussion took place regarding funding options for childcare services in South Nesting. It was RESOLVED: that CDCN would explore the possibility of securing additional funding through various means, including grants and partnerships with local organizations. Proposed by Andrew, seconded by Mark Ratter. Carried.

ACTION: Investigate available funding streams — Jane Smith, by 15 February 2026.

### 5.2 Progress on Bird Hide Project

The board discussed the progress of the bird hide project, which has been delayed due to inclement weather conditions. It was RESOLVED: that the project would continue with a revised timeline and budget adjustments as necessary. Proposed by John Taylor, seconded by Jane Smith. Carried.

ACTION: Review project timeline and budget — Mark Ratter, by 22 February 2026.

### 5.3 Polycrub Rental Update

The director responsible for the Polycrub rental scheme reported that there is currently a waiting list of interested tenants. It was RESOLVED: that CDCN would explore options to increase the number of available rentals and improve marketing efforts. Proposed by Andrew, seconded by John Taylor. Carried.

ACTION: Review rental strategy — Jane Smith, by 01 March 2026.

### 5.4 Gym Meeting with HIE

The board discussed a meeting scheduled for early March 2026 with Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE) regarding funding opportunities for the gym project in South Nesting. It was RESOLVED: that CDCN would prioritize efforts to secure funding through HIE and explore alternative options if necessary. Proposed by Mark Ratter, seconded by Jane Smith. Carried.

ACTION: Follow up with HIE — Andrew, by 10 March 2026.

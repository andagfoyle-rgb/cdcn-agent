# Trustee Report — 2025/26

## Objectives and Activities

### The Aald Skül

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) was founded in December 2016 by local residents who came together to save the former South Nesting Primary School — the Aald Skül — from disposal by Shetland Islands Council (SIC). The group successfully secured the building for community benefit and incorporated as a company limited by guarantee on 22 February 2018 (Company Number 589570). CDCN achieved charitable status on 27 February 2018 (Scottish Charity Number SC048164).

The Aald Skül now serves as a community hub, providing facilities and services that support local residents and contribute to the social and economic wellbeing of South Nesting and the wider area.

### Community Ideas

CDCN operates a membership model that gives local residents a voice in the organisation's direction and priorities. The Community Action Plan, published in September 2024 following extensive community consultation, continues to guide CDCN's work programme. The plan identifies key priorities including housing, childcare, and infrastructure development. CDCN remains committed to community-led development, working with residents, partner organisations, and funders to deliver projects that reflect local needs and aspirations.

### Business Studio

During the year, the Local History Group made use of the Business Studio space to catalogue and organise records stored at the Aald Skül. The board continues to explore options for future use of the Business Studio to generate income and support local enterprise.

### Childcare

Addressing the shortage of childcare provision in the area remains a priority identified in the Community Action Plan. During the year, CDCN continued to seek funding for a childcare study. Two potential funding sources were identified: the Reactive Fund from Scottish Community Business Fund (SCBF) and the Community Local Led Development (CLLD) programme. The board agreed to explore options with the lead contacts of the Nesting Childcare Working Group on how best to formalise the group's structure before proceeding with funding applications.

### Housing

Discussions noted that the Rural and Islands Housing Fund can only be applied for once, meaning substantial research work would need to be completed before making an application.

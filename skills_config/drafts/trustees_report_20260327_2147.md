# Trustee Report — 2025-26

## Objectives and Activities

### The Aald Skül

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) was formed in December 2016 by local residents with the aim of saving the former South Nesting Primary School, known locally as the Aald Skül, from disposal by Shetland Islands Council. The group successfully campaigned to retain the building for community benefit.

CDCN was incorporated as a company limited by guarantee on 22 February 2018 (Company Number 589570) and registered as a Scottish charity on 27 February 2018 (SC048164). The organisation is governed by the Board of Directors and regulated by OSCR (Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator) and Companies House Scotland.

### Community Ideas

CDCN operates a membership model through which local residents can influence the direction and priorities of the organisation. The Community Action Plan 2024–2034, developed with funding from Community Led Local Development (CLLD), outlines the key priorities identified through community consultation. CDCN remains committed to community-led development, responding to local needs and supporting initiatives that enhance wellbeing, sustainability, and economic resilience in South Nesting and the surrounding area.

## Achievements and Performance

### Growing Spaces and Polycrubs

The Growing Spaces project continued to provide community growing opportunities through the rental of polycrubs at the Aald Skül site. Demand remained strong throughout the year, with a waiting list maintained for prospective tenants. The project supports local food production and contributes to community resilience and wellbeing.

### Bird Hide

Maintenance work was undertaken at the Bird Hide during the year, including repairs to the handrail and weeding of access paths. The site remains a valued community asset for wildlife observation and enjoyment of the local environment.

### Nesting Scrapstore

The Scrapstore continued operations from its premises at the Aald Skül, providing salvage materials and resources for community projects and creative reuse. Plant sales generated approximately £200 during the reporting period. Planning progressed for the installation of energy-efficient LED lighting within the Scrapstore building, supported by external funding.

### Community Heritage Trail

A funding application was developed for the South Nesting Community Heritage Trail, with a request for £15,000 submitted to Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE). The project aims to create an engaging heritage trail showcasing South Nesting's history through signage, lighting, and access pathways. A meeting was held with HIE on 6 March 2026 regarding the application, with approval anticipated by the end of March 2026.

### Energy Efficiency and Solar PV Installation

CDCN secured funding from CARES Scotland and the Shetland Community Benefit Fund for the installation of solar panels and battery storage at the Aald Skül. The installation progressed during the year, reducing energy costs and contributing to the organisation's sustainability objectives. An application for a feed-in tariff was in preparation.

### Community Resilience

Funding was secured from the SSEN Community Resilience Fund for the acquisition of a portable generator to support the community during power outages. The South Nesting Public Hall is being developed as a resilience hub for emergency situations.

### Housing Initiatives

CDCN continued to explore options for addressing local housing needs. Discussions took place regarding the Rural Islands Housing Fund, and the organisation contributed to broader conversations about affordable housing provision in the area. A housing needs assessment remained under consideration.

### South Nesting Public Hall

CDCN provided ongoing support to South Nesting Public Hall, including coordination on resilience planning and facility improvements. The hall serves as an important community venue and complements the activities based at the Aald Skül.

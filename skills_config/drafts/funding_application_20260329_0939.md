## 1. Organisation Overview

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) is a Scottish charitable organisation and company limited by guarantee, based in South Nesting, Shetland. CDCN is governed by OSCR (Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator) and registered with Companies House Scotland. The organisation was established to serve the communities of South Nesting and the wider Nesting area.

CDCN operates from **The Aald Skül**, the former South Nesting Primary School, which the organisation has developed as a community hub hosting a range of services and facilities. Current activities include the **Nesting Scrapstore** (providing salvage materials for community projects), **Growing Spaces** (Polycrubs for community growers), a community gym, and support for local heritage initiatives.

The organisation has successfully secured and managed funding from multiple sources including Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE), the Shetland Community Benefit Fund, and CARES Scotland. CDCN has a track record of delivering community infrastructure projects, including solar PV and battery storage installations, a bird hide development, and energy efficiency upgrades to community facilities.

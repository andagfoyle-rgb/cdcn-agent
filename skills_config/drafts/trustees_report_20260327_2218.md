# Trustee Report — 2025–2026

## Objectives and Activities

### The Aald Skül

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) was founded in December 2016 by local residents seeking to save the former South Nesting Primary School, known locally as the Aald Skül, from disposal by Shetland Islands Council. The organisation was incorporated on 22 February 2018 (Company Number 589570) and registered as a Scottish charity on 27 February 2018 (SC048164).

The Aald Skül remains CDCN's primary asset and serves as a community hub, providing space for local groups, enterprises and community activities. The building houses the Nesting Scrapstore, the Business Studio, and facilities available for community hire.

### Community Ideas

CDCN operates a membership model open to all residents of the Nesting area aged 16 and over. The organisation remains committed to community-led development, guided by the priorities identified in the Nesting, Girlsta & Wadbister Community Action Plan 2024–2034. This plan was developed through community consultation and establishes the strategic framework for CDCN's work programme.

The board continues to welcome ideas and input from members and the wider community, recognising that sustainable development must be shaped by local needs and aspirations.

## 1. Organisation Overview

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) is a Scottish charitable organisation and company limited by guarantee, based in South Nesting, Shetland. Founded in December 2016 by local residents, CDCN was established to save the former South Nesting Primary School — the Aald Skül — from disposal by Shetland Islands Council (SIC). The organisation successfully secured the building for community benefit and has since developed it into a thriving community hub.

CDCN serves the communities of South Nesting, Girlsta, and Wadbister, delivering a range of services and facilities that respond to local needs. Current operations include the Nesting Scrapstore, community growing spaces (Polycrubs), a community gym, and various rental facilities. The organisation employs a Development Officer on a part-time basis and is governed by a Board of Directors drawn from the local community. Key priorities identified through community consultation include housing, childcare, and infrastructure development, as documented in the Nesting, Girlsta and Wadbister Community Action Plan 2024.

## 2. Project Description

CDCN seeks funding to commission a consultant to undertake a feasibility study and options appraisal for affordable housing in South Nesting. The project will deliver a comprehensive housing needs assessment and an associated business plan, providing the evidence base and strategic framework required to progress affordable housing development in the area.

The project will be delivered over approximately six months and will involve the following activities:

- **Housing needs assessment:** Engaging with the local community to identify current and projected housing requirements, including affordable housing needs, downsizing options for older residents, and housing for young families and workers.
- **Feasibility study:** Assessing potential sites for housing development within South Nesting, considering land availability, infrastructure requirements, planning constraints, and community support.
- **Options appraisal:** Evaluating different delivery models for affordable housing, including community-led housing, partnership approaches with housing associations, and other viable models.
- **Business plan:** Developing a practical, costed plan for taking forward the preferred option, including outline budgets, funding sources, governance requirements, and an implementation timeline.

The consultant will work closely with CDCN's Board of Directors and Development Officer throughout the project, and will engage with key stakeholders including Shetland Islands Council (SIC), Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE), and local residents.

## 5. Evaluation Approach

CDCN will evaluate the project's success through clear output measures. The primary evidence of success will be the completion and acceptance of the four key documents: housing needs assessment, feasibility study, options appraisal, and business plan. The Board of Directors will formally review and approve each document, ensuring that deliverables meet the agreed scope and quality standards.

Community engagement will be documented through attendance records at consultation events and a summary of responses received. The consultant will be required to provide evidence of community participation and demonstrate how local views have informed the needs assessment and options appraisal. Upon project completion, CDCN will hold a community presentation to share findings and gather feedback, providing an additional measure of community engagement and satisfaction with the process.

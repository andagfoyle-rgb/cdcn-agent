## 1. Organisation Overview

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) is a Scottish charitable organisation and company limited by guarantee, based in South Nesting, Shetland. Governed by OSCR (Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator) and Companies House Scotland, CDCN was established in 2016 to save the former South Nesting Primary School—known locally as The Aald Skül—from disposal. Today, the building serves as a vital community hub hosting the Nesting Scrapstore, Growing Spaces (Polycrubs), a community gym, business units, and heritage facilities.

CDCN has a strong track record of delivering capital projects that improve local amenities and resilience. Recent achievements include the installation of solar PV and battery storage (funded by CARES Scotland and the Shetland Community Benefit Fund), the development of a community bird hide, and the expansion of the Scrapstore via a new portacabin (funded by the Islands Programme via LEADER/CLLD). Notably, CDCN has an existing positive funding relationship with SUEZ, who contributed approximately £39,693 (21% of build costs) towards the original gym refurbishment completed in 2019/20. This prior investment successfully established a fitness facility that now requires expansion to meet growing demand.

## 2. Project Description

**This project will significantly enhance health and wellbeing in South Nesting by alleviating critical capacity constraints at our community gym, ensuring residents can access cardiovascular fitness facilities locally without travelling to Lerwick.**

Currently, the Nesting Gym operates from a single room within The Aald Skül, housing free weights alongside limited cardio equipment. With membership now approaching 300 individuals, the space is frequently congested, limiting floor space and accessibility. To address this, CDCN proposes installing a dedicated portacabin on-site to create a separate Cardio Gym Facility.

In December 2025, the gym management group surveyed the top 30 users, presenting three options for development. Option 3—the creation of a two-room setup with one room dedicated to cardio equipment—received the strongest support, confirming clear community demand for this solution.

The new facility will be positioned adjacent to the existing gym to minimise cabling runs and allow convenient access. It will be fitted out with three or four pieces of commercial-grade cardio equipment, likely including treadmills, cross-trainers, and rowing machines, with the final mix determined by a follow-up user survey. This interim solution provides a cost-effective immediate response to capacity issues while CDCN develops long-term plans for a larger purpose-built facility.

**Budget Narrative**

The budget request of **£40,000 – £44,000** covers the total capital expenditure required to deliver this facility.

*   **Portacabin (£30,000):** This represents the largest single cost. We have sourced indicative costs for a new, insulated unit suitable for year-round use in Shetland’s climate. This approach mirrors our successful Scrapstore expansion model (delivered 2023) and offers a faster, more flexible alternative to traditional brick-and-mortar extension at this stage.
*   **Equipment (£8,000 – £12,000):** Commercial-grade machinery is essential to withstand heavy daily use by up to 300 members. This allocation allows us to purchase reliable brands with available service support, reducing long-term maintenance liability compared to domestic-spec equipment.
*   **Fit-out (£1,500 – £2,000):** Proper rubber flooring is a non-negotiable requirement for sound dampening and joint protection, ensuring the facility meets health and safety standards for physical activity.

All figures include VAT where applicable. Any underspend on equipment will be reallocated to additional fit-out requirements or high-quality storage solutions for the space.

## 5. Evaluation Approach

CDCN will monitor the success of this project through quantitative usage data and qualitative feedback:

*   **Usage Metrics:** We will utilise the existing gym booking system (SumUp/online log) to track session attendance in the new cardio room separately from the weights room. This data will show utilisation rates and peak times.
*   **Membership Growth:** The Development Officer and gym volunteers will monitor new inductions and membership renewals to identify trends directly attributable to the improved facilities.
*   **User Satisfaction:** Six months after opening, we will conduct a survey of active users. This will assess satisfaction with the equipment selection, the environment (heating/lighting), and perceived impact on their fitness routines.
*   **Operational Review:** The Board of Directors will review energy consumption (heating/electricity) for the new unit in the annual accounts to ensure ongoing sustainability.

CDCN values the continued support of SUEZ in developing community infrastructure in South Nesting. This application builds directly upon the success of SUEZ’s previous contribution to the Nesting Gym.

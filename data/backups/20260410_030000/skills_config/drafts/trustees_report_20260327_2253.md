# Trustee Report — 2025–26

## Objectives and Activities

### The Aald Skül

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) was founded in December 2016 by local residents seeking to save the former South Nesting Primary School — known locally as the Aald Skül — from disposal by Shetland Islands Council. The group successfully campaigned to retain the building for community benefit, and CDCN was incorporated as a company limited by guarantee on 22 February 2018 (Company Number 589570). The organisation achieved charitable status on 27 February 2018 (Scottish Charity Number SC048164) and is regulated by OSCR.

The Aald Skül continues to serve as CDCN's operational base and primary community asset, housing a range of activities and services developed in response to local needs.

### Community Ideas

CDCN operates as a membership-based organisation, guided by the principle of community-led development. The organisation maintains an ongoing commitment to consulting with residents and responding to locally identified priorities. This approach is reflected in the Community Action Plan and the regular community surveys that inform CDCN's work programme. Membership is open to all residents of the Ness area, and the board continues to welcome new ideas and proposals from the community.

## Achievements and Performance

### Scrapstore

The Nesting Scrapstore has continued to operate throughout the year, providing salvage materials and creative resources for community projects. The Scrapstore has maintained its popular plant sales, generating approximately £200 during the reporting period, and continues to serve as a valued community facility. The storefront space remains active with consistent engagement from local residents and volunteers.

During the year, the Scrapstore management structure underwent a significant change. Vic Thomas, who had been job-sharing the Scrapstore Team Leader role with Chloe Hansen, retired from the position. Chloe Hansen has now taken on the full role. Additionally, Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE) funding for the Scrapstore manager post will not be extended beyond the current period, and CDCN has taken the decision to fund the position from its own resources to ensure continuity of this valued community service.

## Rules

- Tidy up and disinfect at end of session
- Always use safety rails for any exercise that carries a risk of entrapment
- Barbell and dumb-bell exercises on rubber mats only
- Always use treadmill safety cord
- Beware low ceiling
- Keep phone handy when training alone in case of emergency
- Report faults/problems
- Adhere to time slots
- Do not pull pulley cables over to side
- Do not hang weights on the stack pin
- No admittance to non-members
- No children/infants
- Keep music to reasonable level – consider the neighbors

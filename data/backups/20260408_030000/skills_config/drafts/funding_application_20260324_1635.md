## 1. Organisation Overview

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) is a Scottish charitable organisation and company limited by guarantee, based in South Nesting, Shetland. Founded in December 2016 by local residents who came together to save the former South Nesting Primary School — the Aald Skül — from disposal by Shetland Islands Council (SIC), CDCN successfully secured the building for community benefit.

CDCN serves the communities of South Nesting and the wider Nesting, Girlsta and Wadbister area. The organisation manages a range of community facilities and services from the Aald Skül site, including the Nesting Scrapstore, community growing spaces (Polycrubs), a community gym, and various rental spaces for local groups and businesses. CDCN employs a Development Officer and team leaders, supported by volunteers.

The organisation has a track record of delivering community projects, including solar panel and battery installations for energy resilience, and continues to pursue initiatives aligned with the priorities identified in the Nesting, Girlsta and Wadbister Community Action Plan 2024.

## 2. Project Description

CDCN seeks funding to improve the energy efficiency and facilities offered at the Aald Skül community building. The project will upgrade essential equipment to reduce operational costs, improve reliability, and enhance services available to the community.

The proposed improvements include:

- **New printer:** Replacement of outdated equipment to improve administrative efficiency and reduce maintenance costs.
- **Commercial washer and dryer:** Installation of energy-efficient laundry equipment to support community facility operations and rental activities.
- **Gym equipment:** Upgrade of fitness equipment to maintain and improve the community gym offering.
- **Electrical works in the Scrapstore:** Installation of LED lighting and two heaters on inverters connected directly to the solar panels, allowing the building to remain lit and heated during power outages.
- **Generator change-over switch:** Installation to improve resilience and backup power capability.

## 5. Evaluation Approach

CDCN will evaluate the success of this project through measurable indicators tracked over the 12 months following installation. These will include:

- Monitoring energy consumption before and after installation to quantify efficiency savings.
- Recording attendance and usage figures for the gym and laundry facilities.
- Tracking maintenance incidents and costs to assess reliability improvements.
- Gathering user feedback through surveys to measure satisfaction with upgraded facilities.

The Development Officer will compile progress reports for the Board of Directors, with a final evaluation report submitted to the funder at project completion.

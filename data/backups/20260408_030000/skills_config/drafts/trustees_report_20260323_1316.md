# Trustee Report — 2025/26

## Objectives and Activities

### The Aald Skül

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) was founded in December 2016 by local residents who came together to save the former South Nesting Primary School — the Aald Skül — from disposal by Shetland Islands Council (SIC). The group successfully secured the building for community benefit and incorporated as a company limited by guarantee on 22 February 2018 (Company Number 589570). CDCN was entered onto the Scottish Charity Register on 27 February 2018 (Charity Number SC048164).

The Aald Skül now serves as a community hub, providing facilities including the Nesting Scrapstore, the Nesting Gym, business units, growing spaces, and venues for community groups and activities. The building continues to be a focal point for community life in South Nesting and the wider area.

### Community Ideas

CDCN remains committed to community-led development. The organisation operates a membership model, giving local residents a direct voice in shaping priorities and activities. This approach is underpinned by the Nesting, Girlsta and Wadbister Community Action Plan 2024–2034, which was completed and published in September 2024. The Action Plan sets out clear priorities for the area, including housing, childcare, and community cohesion, and guides CDCN's work programme and discussions with external partners and funders.

## Achievements and Performance

### Development Officer

A new Development Officer was appointed during the year and commenced work at the end of July 2025. The role is funded for 25 hours per week. Ensuring continuity and an effective handover required considerable planning by the board and staff. In March 2026, CDCN held a funding meeting with Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE) to secure grant funding for the Development Officer position for a further 12 months from April 2026 to March 2027. The grant application was submitted and HIE indicated approval would be in place before March 2026, with a grant allocation of £6,175.

### Nesting Scrapstore

The Scrapstore continued operations throughout the year, providing salvage materials for community projects. The store remained popular, with volunteers and staff supporting its day-to-day running. The board discussed ways to improve transparency around Scrapstore income, with a suggestion that a straightforward explanation could be included as an addendum to the annual accounts published on the website.

### Nesting Gym

The gym management team continued to operate the facility throughout the year. In December 2025, an assault bike was ordered for the gym. The board held a meeting scheduled for early March 2026 with Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE) regarding funding opportunities for the gym project in South Nesting, with a focus on the Rural Retail Fund. CDCN agreed to prioritise efforts to secure funding through HIE and explore alternative options if necessary.

### Housing

The board continued to explore options for affordable housing in the area. It was noted that the Rural and Islands Housing Fund can only be applied for once, so considerable research work would need to be completed beforehand. The board agreed to seek other sources of funding for a Housing Needs Assessment and associated preliminary data gathering. ### Childcare

CDCN supported the Nesting Childcare Working Group during the year.

# CDCN Agent — Style Guide

CDCN is Community Development Company Nesting, a Scottish charitable organisation and company limited by guarantee, based in South Nesting, Shetland. Governed by OSCR (Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator) and Companies House Scotland.

This guide is maintained by the board and updated as preferences evolve. It supplements the writer templates in `skills_config/writer_templates/`.

---

## Tone and Register

- Professional, warm, and community-focused throughout
- Active voice preferred; passive voice only where the subject is genuinely unknown
- Write for a general reader — assume no specialist knowledge unless the document is addressed to a specialist audience (e.g. auditors, OSCR)
- Avoid jargon; spell out acronyms in full on first use, then abbreviate
- Refer to the organisation as "CDCN" after the first full mention of "Community Development Company Nesting"
- Standard conventions: dates DD Month YYYY, currency £X,XXX sterling, numbers one to nine spelled out, 10 and above as numerals

## Preferred Structures for Funding Applications

- Lead with a two-sentence impact statement before any description of activities
- State measurable, time-bound outcomes (numbers of beneficiaries, outputs, dates)
- Reference prior achievements with evidence where available
- Name community partners explicitly — do not write "various local organisations"
- Budget figures must be in £ sterling and must add up correctly
- Close with a statement of organisational capacity relevant to the funder's priorities

## Board Communication Preferences

- Board minutes: past tense, third person, decisions and actions only — not the detail of debate
- Resolutions format: **RESOLVED:** [decision]. Proposed by [name]. Seconded by [name]. **Carried** / **Not carried.**
- Action points format: **ACTION:** [task description] — [name responsible], by [target date]
- Draft minutes to be circulated within five working days of the meeting
- Board papers: concise cover note (one page maximum) followed by the substantive document

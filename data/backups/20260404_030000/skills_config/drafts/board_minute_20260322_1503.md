## Agenda Items

### 5.1 Funding Options for Childcare Services

The board discussed various options for funding childcare services in Nesting. RESOLVED: to explore partnership with local authorities for potential grant funding. Proposed by Jane Smith, seconded by Mark Ratter. Carried.
ACTION: Research potential grants — Sarah Johnson by 2026-02-15.

### 5.2 Bird Hide Progress Update

The board received an update on the bird hide project, which has been impacted by weather conditions. RESOLVED: to delay the project timeline due to unforeseen circumstances. Proposed by Andrew Taylor, seconded by Emily Patel. Carried.
ACTION: Review project schedule — Jane Smith by 2026-02-20.

### 5.3 Polycrub Rental Update

The board discussed ongoing rentals for the Polycrub and noted that there is a waiting list. RESOLVED: to explore additional rental options to meet demand. Proposed by Mark Ratter, seconded by Sarah Johnson. Carried.
ACTION: Investigate alternative rental arrangements — Emily Patel by 2026-02-22.

### 5.4 Gym Meeting Update

The board discussed the upcoming gym meeting focused on rural retail fund accessibility and concerns about government voucher misuse. RESOLVED: to ensure that CDCN's policies align with government guidelines on voucher use. Proposed by Jane Smith, seconded by Andrew Taylor. Carried.
ACTION: Review policies — Mark Ratter by 2026-02-25.

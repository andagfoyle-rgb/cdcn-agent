## 1. Organisation Overview

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) is a Scottish charitable organisation and company limited by guarantee, based in South Nesting, Shetland. CDCN is governed by OSCR (Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator) and registered with Companies House Scotland.

CDCN was established to manage community assets and deliver development projects for the benefit of residents in South Nesting and the wider Shetland community. The organisation's primary asset is The Aald Skül, the former South Nesting Primary School, which serves as a community hub hosting various groups and activities.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the principles and procedures governing the use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools by Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN). It exists to ensure that AI is used ethically, safely, and effectively in support of CDCN's charitable objectives, while managing the risks associated with AI-generated content and automated processes. The policy addresses the need for human oversight, data protection, and transparency in all AI-assisted activities, in line with Scottish Government guidance on AI governance and CDCN's obligations as a registered Scottish charity regulated by OSCR.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All members of the Board of Directors
- All employees of CDCN
- Volunteers working on behalf of CDCN
- Contractors and consultants engaged by CDCN who use AI tools in the course of their work

The policy covers use of:

- CDCN Agent, the AI assistant configured for CDCN's use
- Any other AI tools, including generative AI services, used for CDCN business

This policy does not apply to personal use of AI tools unconnected with CDCN activities.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| AI (Artificial Intelligence) | Software systems that can perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence, such as understanding language, analysing data, or generating text. |
| CDCN Agent | The specific AI assistant created for Community Development Company Nesting, configured to understand and work with CDCN documents and priorities. |

## 4. Policy Statement

### 4.1 Commitment to Ethical AI Use

CDCN is committed to using AI tools in a manner that upholds its charitable objectives, serves the community of South Nesting, and maintains public trust. AI will be used to enhance operational efficiency, support decision-making with better information, and free up volunteer directors and staff for work requiring human judgment and community engagement.

### 4.2 Human Oversight and Accountability

All AI-generated outputs require human review and sign-off before being used, published, or acted upon. AI does not replace human judgment, and responsibility for decisions remains with the Board, individual directors, and staff as appropriate. CDCN Agent cannot make decisions on behalf of the organisation.

### 4.3 Transparency

CDCN will maintain a log of AI systems used for organisational purposes. Documents produced with significant AI assistance will be clearly marked as drafts requiring review and approval. The Board will be informed of how AI tools are being used within the organisation.

### 4.4 Data Protection and Confidentiality

AI tools must be used in compliance with data protection legislation and CDCN's existing confidentiality and data protection policies. Sensitive personal data, confidential business information, and matters subject to legal privilege should not be entered into external AI systems. CDCN Agent has been configured to operate within a secure environment for processing CDCN documents.

### 4.5 Accuracy and Verification

AI-generated content may contain errors, omissions, or outdated information. All users are responsible for verifying important facts, figures, and references before relying on AI outputs. This is particularly important for funding applications, financial documents, and public communications.

### 4.6 Bias and Fairness

CDCN will not use AI to make decisions that could result in unfair treatment of individuals or community groups. AI outputs relating to community needs, service provision, or prioritisation of projects must be reviewed to guard against bias.

### 4.7 Compliance with Legal and Regulatory Requirements

Use of AI will comply with all applicable legislation, including data protection law, charity law, and company law. CDCN will follow Scottish Government guidance on AI governance as it evolves.

## 6. Procedures

### 6.1 Approved Uses of AI

CDCN Agent and other approved AI tools may be used for:

1. Searching and summarising CDCN's archive of minutes, policies, and other documents
2. Drafting agendas, minutes, reports, and correspondence for subsequent human review
3. Preparing draft funding applications and reports, drawing on organisational information
4. Summarising policies, procedures, or background documents
5. Identifying information about past decisions, projects, or contacts stored in the archive

The following uses are prohibited:

- Entering sensitive personal data into external AI systems not specifically approved for such use
- Using AI to make final decisions on funding, employment, or service provision without human review
- Publishing or distributing AI-generated content without appropriate review and approval
- Using AI in ways that could breach confidentiality obligations

### 6.2 Review and Approval Process

1. **Draft production:** When CDCN Agent or another AI tool produces a draft document, it must be clearly labelled as a draft requiring review.
2. **Human review:** A director, staff member, or volunteer with appropriate knowledge must review the draft for accuracy, appropriateness, and completeness.
3. **Verification:** Any facts, figures, dates, or names must be verified against original sources where the information is material.
4. **Amendment:** The reviewer may amend the draft directly or request revisions.
5. **Approval:** For board papers, funding applications, public communications, and formal documents, approval must be given by an appropriate person (typically a director or the Board collectively).
6. **Sign-off:** The final approved document should indicate who reviewed and approved it.

### 6.3 Logging and Record-Keeping

1. CDCN will maintain a record of AI systems used for organisational purposes, including:
   - Name of the AI tool
   - Purpose for which it is used
   - Data types it is permitted to process
   - Date of approval for use
   - Person responsible for oversight

2. The Secretary will maintain this log and present it for Board review annually.

3. Significant AI-related incidents (such as data breaches, errors in published content, or misuse) must be reported to a director promptly and recorded in the incident log.

### 6.4 Handling Concerns or Incidents

1. Anyone who becomes aware of misuse of AI tools, data breaches involving AI, or errors in AI-generated outputs that have been published or acted upon should report this to a director as soon as practicable.
2. The director receiving the report will assess the severity of the incident and determine appropriate next steps, which may include:
   - Correcting any published errors
   - Notifying affected individuals if personal data is involved
   - Reviewing and amending procedures to prevent recurrence
   - Reporting to the Board at the next meeting
3. Serious incidents will be recorded and considered at the Board's regular review of this policy.

## 7. Monitoring and Compliance

Compliance with this policy will be monitored as follows:

- The Development Officer or Development Consultant will provide a brief update on AI use at each Board meeting, including any issues arising.
- The Board will review this policy and the log of AI systems used at least annually.
- Any breach of this policy will be addressed through CDCN's existing disciplinary or grievance procedures as appropriate to the individual concerned.
- Serious breaches may result in withdrawal of access to AI tools and reporting requirements.

Compliance with this policy is mandatory for all individuals within its scope. Failure to follow the policy may:

- Compromise data protection compliance and expose CDCN to regulatory action
- Result in publication of inaccurate information, damaging CDCN's reputation
- Undermine trust in CDCN's governance and decision-making processes

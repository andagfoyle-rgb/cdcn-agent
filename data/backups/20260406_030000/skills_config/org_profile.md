## Charitable Objects

CDCN exists to work in co-operation with organisations and individuals within and beyond Nesting for:

1. **Community development and resilience** — including rural regeneration, by designing and delivering services, volunteering and learning opportunities to help Nesting be a vibrant, sustainable and inclusive community
2. **Heritage and culture** — raising awareness of the Nesting community, its historic and natural environment, and its traditions and culture
3. **Recreational facilities** — improving conditions of life to encourage people to settle and remain in the community
4. **Relief of those in need** — by reason of age, ill-health, disability, financial hardship or other disadvantage through accessible and freely available services

## Community Context

CDCN operates in the South Nesting area of Shetland, serving a rural community covering North Nesting, South Nesting, Girlsta & Wadbister. The area is characterised by dispersed settlement, limited public transport, and strong community identity. CDCN works alongside Shetland Islands Council (SIC), Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE), DTAS, and other local and national bodies to support community development.

Key assets:
- **Aald Skül** (Old School, Vassa) — community hub housing gym, scrapstore, business studio, meeting spaces
- **Bird Hide** — community amenity
- **Scrapstore** — retail operation with portacabin extension
- **Gym** — membership-based fitness facility
- **Storage units / Polycrubs** — community rental
- **Solar PV panels** — installed on roof (Nordri project)

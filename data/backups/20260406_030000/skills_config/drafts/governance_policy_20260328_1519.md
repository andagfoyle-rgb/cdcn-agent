## What is CDCN Agent?

CDCN Agent is an artificial intelligence assistant designed specifically for Community Development Company Nesting. It understands plain English questions and helps with the administrative tasks that take up valuable volunteer time.

Think of it as a knowledgeable colleague who has read all your minutes, policies, funding applications, and correspondence — and can quickly find information, draft documents, or remind you of important deadlines. It works through simple conversations, just like sending a message to a colleague.

---

## Why Was It Created?

The board identified four key reasons for developing CDCN Agent:

> **Reducing Administrative Burden on Volunteers**
> Volunteers give their time to support the community, but find themselves spending hours on paperwork, searching through old files, and preparing meeting materials. CDCN Agent handles these tasks so volunteers can focus on what matters.

> **Improving Access to Organisational Information**
> Important decisions, precedents, and institutional knowledge are often buried in meeting minutes or email chains. CDCN Agent makes this information instantly searchable and accessible.

> **Supporting Funding Applications and Tracking**
> Funding applications require significant effort, and tracking multiple deadlines can be challenging. CDCN Agent helps identify opportunities, draft applications, and monitor key dates.

> **Preserving Institutional Memory Across Board Changes**
> When board members change, valuable knowledge about past decisions and ongoing projects can be lost. CDCN Agent retains this information and makes it available to new directors.

## What Can It Do?

### 🔍 Search the Archive

Find information from minutes, policies, funding applications, and other documents. Ask a question in plain English and receive a relevant answer with source references.

**Example:** *What did we decide about the defibrillator funding?*

---

### 📝 Draft Documents

Create minutes, policies, reports, funding applications, and agendas. CDCN Agent follows organisational templates and style guides automatically.

**Example:** *Draft an agenda for next week's board meeting.*

---

### 💰 Check Funding

Identify funding opportunities, track application deadlines, and review grant requirements. Stay on top of multiple funding streams with automated reminders.

**Example:** *What funding deadlines are coming up in the next three months?*

---

### 🧠 Manage Memory

Store and retrieve key decisions, contact details, and institutional knowledge. Build an organisational memory that persists across board changes.

**Example:** *What contacts do we have at Highlands and Islands Enterprise?*

## What CDCN Agent Cannot Do

It is important to understand the limitations of CDCN Agent. The following table sets out what it cannot do:

| Limitation | What This Means |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Cannot make decisions** | All decisions remain with the Board of Directors. CDCN Agent provides information and drafts, but authority always rests with human directors. |
| **Cannot replace human judgment** | Use CDCN Agent as a support tool, not a substitute for your own expertise and knowledge of the community. |
| **Cannot access paper-only documents** | CDCN Agent works with digitised archive content only. Documents held only in paper form are not available to it. |
| **Cannot guarantee accuracy** | Always verify important details against source documents. CDCN Agent can make mistakes — check before acting. |
| **Cannot browse live websites** | CDCN Agent works with archived documents only. It cannot check current websites or real-time information. |
| **Cannot send emails autonomously** | Any drafts produced must be reviewed and sent by a human. CDCN Agent does not send communications on your behalf. |

> **Remember:** CDCN Agent is a tool to support your work, not replace the judgment and authority of the Board. All outputs should be reviewed before use.

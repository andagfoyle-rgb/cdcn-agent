## 1. Organisation Overview

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) is a Scottish charitable organisation and company limited by guarantee, based in South Nesting, Shetland. CDCN is governed by OSCR (Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator) and registered with Companies House Scotland. The organisation was founded in December 2016 by local residents who came together to save the former South Nesting Primary School — the Aald Skül — from disposal by Shetland Islands Council. CDCN successfully secured the building for community benefit and incorporated as a company limited by guarantee on 22 February 2018.

CDCN operates from The Aald Skül, which now serves as a community hub providing facilities, services, and activities for the benefit of residents in South Nesting and the wider Nesting area. Current activities include the Nesting Scrapstore (providing salvage materials for community projects), Growing Spaces (Polycrubs for community growers), a community gym, and support for local heritage initiatives. The organisation has a track record of successfully securing and managing funding from multiple sources including Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE), the Shetland Community Benefit Fund, Scottish Land Fund, LEADER, and CARES Scotland.

CDCN has delivered community infrastructure projects including the renovation of the former school building, solar PV and battery storage installations, a bird hide development, and energy efficiency upgrades to community facilities. The organisation works in close partnership with South Nesting Hall, Shetland Islands Council, local community groups, and other development trusts across Shetland.

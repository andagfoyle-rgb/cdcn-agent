## 1. Organisation Overview

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) is a Scottish charitable organisation and company limited by guarantee, based in South Nesting, Shetland. Governed by OSCR (Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator) and Companies House Scotland, CDCN was established to save the former South Nesting Primary School—known locally as The Aald Skül—and develop it as a community hub.

Since its formation in 2016, CDCN has successfully delivered key infrastructure projects including the Nesting Scrapstore, Growing Spaces (Polycrubs), and the Nesting Gym. The organisation has a strong track record of securing and managing funding from bodies such as Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE), the National Lottery Community Fund, and Shetland Islands Council (SIC). The Nesting Gym currently serves approximately 300 members and acts as a vital asset for community health and well-being in the area.

---

## 2. Project Description

This project proposes the installation of a dedicated **Portacabin Cardio Facility** at the Aald Skül site to expand the fitness offering of the Nesting Gym.

Currently, the main gym space houses both free weights and cardio equipment. As membership grows, this has led to congestion and limitations on floor space. While a new purpose-built gym facility (estimated cost £300,000–£350,000) remains a long-term objective, CDCN proposes this interim solution to meet immediate demand cost-effectively.

The facility will be located in a portacabin on-site and will contain **three or four pieces of cardio equipment**. Based on initial discussions, this is likely to include:
*   One or two treadmills.
*   Two or three additional cardio machines (e.g., cross-trainers, rowing machines, or exercise bikes).

**Critical Next Step:**
Before finalising the equipment purchase, CDCN must conduct a **survey of the top 30 most active gym users**. This consultation will determine exactly which machines the membership prioritises, ensuring the investment matches user demand. This aligns with the preference for "Option 3" (a gym with two rooms, one dedicated to cardio) identified in recent member surveys.

---

## 3. Intended Outcomes

*   **Increased Capacity:** Separate cardio accommodation frees up space in the main gym hall, allowing for better layout of free weights and reducing overcrowding during peak times.
*   **Improved User Experience:** Members benefit from a wider range of equipment without waiting times, potentially driving membership retention and growth beyond the current 300 members.
*   **Health and Wellbeing:** Provides residents of South Nesting and the wider Nesting area with improved access to physical activity facilities, supporting CDCN’s charitable aim of advancing health.
*   **Operational Safety:** Physically separating heavy weightlifting zones from high-intensity cardio zones reduces the risk of injury.

## 5. Evaluation Approach

CDCN will measure the success of this project through the following methods:

1.  **Pre-Installation Survey:** A targeted survey of the top 30 gym users to select the specific machines to be purchased. This ensures the capital expenditure directly reflects user needs.
2.  **Usage Data Analysis:** Post-installation, CDCN will monitor booking system data to track utilisation rates of the new portacabin compared to the main gym hall.
3.  **Membership Feedback:** Informal feedback collection during inductions and via the gym management team to assess satisfaction with the new equipment variety.

# Trustee Report — 2025-26

## Objectives and Activities

### The Aald Skül

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) was founded in December 2016 by local residents following the decision by Shetland Islands Council to dispose of the former South Nesting Primary School. The community came together to save the building,_known locally as the Aald Skül, for community benefit. CDCN was incorporated as a company limited by guarantee on 22 February 2018 (Company Number 589570) and registered as a Scottish charity on 27 February 2018 (Scottish Charity Number SC048164). The organisation is governed by OSCR (Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator) and Companies House Scotland.

The Aald Skül remains CDCN's primary community asset and serves as a hub for local groups, activities, and services. CDCN continues to develop the site in line with community priorities identified through consultation and the Community Action Plan.

### Community Ideas

CDCN operates a membership model open to all residents of South Nesting, ensuring community-led development remains at the heart of the organisation's work. The membership has contributed to shaping priorities through community surveys and consultation processes. CDCN remains committed to supporting projects and services that reflect local needs and aspirations, with ongoing engagement through the Community Action Plan 2024–2034.

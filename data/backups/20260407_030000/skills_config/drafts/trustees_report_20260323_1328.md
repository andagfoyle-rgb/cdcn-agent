# Trustee Report — 2025/26

## Objectives and Activities

### The Aald Skül

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) was founded in December 2016 by local residents who came together to save the former South Nesting Primary School — the Aald Skül — from disposal by Shetland Islands Council (SIC). The group successfully secured the building for community benefit and incorporated as a company limited by guarantee on 22 February 2018 (Company Number 589570). CDCN was entered on the Scottish Charity Register on 27 February 2018 (Charity Number SC048164).

The Aald Skül now serves as a community hub, housing the Nesting Scrapstore, gym facilities, business studio, and growing spaces. The building provides a base for CDCN's activities and a venue for community groups and events.

### Community Ideas

CDCN operates a membership model, giving local residents a voice in the organisation's priorities and direction. The Community Action Plan for Nesting, Girlsta and Wadbister, published in September 2024, provides a framework for community-led development and guides CDCN's work programme. Priority themes identified through community consultation include housing, childcare, and infrastructure development.

# Trustee Report — 2025–26

## Objectives and Activities

### The Aald Skül

Community Development Company Nesting was founded in December 2016 by local residents determined to save the former South Nesting Primary School from disposal by Shetland Islands Council. The building, known locally as the Aald Skül, was secured for community benefit through the efforts of the founding members and has since served as a hub for local activities and services.

CDCN was incorporated as a company limited by guarantee on 22 February 2018 (Company Number 589570) and registered as a Scottish charity on 27 February 2018 (SC048164). The organisation is governed by a Board of Directors elected from the membership, with regulatory oversight from OSCR (Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator) and Companies House Scotland.

The Aald Skül remains CDCN's primary asset and the focus of ongoing development efforts to maximise its benefit to the community of South Nesting and the wider Shetland area.

### Community Ideas

CDCN continues to operate a community-driven approach to development. The organisation maintains an open membership model, allowing residents to participate in shaping local priorities. The Community Action Plan 2024–2034, developed through stakeholder conversations, focus groups, and public consultation, provides the strategic framework for CDCN's work programme.

The Board remains committed to community-led development, seeking to address needs identified through ongoing engagement with residents, partner organisations, and local stakeholders.

### Energy Efficiency and Solar PV

Significant progress was made on energy efficiency projects during the year. CDCN secured funding from CARES Scotland and the Shetland Community Benefit Fund for the installation of solar panels and battery storage at the Aald Skül. Building Warrant approval was obtained for the installation works.

The electricity supply contract with Octopus Energy was reviewed during the period, following identification of a large backdated bill. The Board approved an application for a feed-in tariff in relation to the solar PV installation. The PV and battery installation is intended to power potential electric vehicle charge points and contribute to the site's sustainability.

### Community Resilience

CDCN received funding from the SSEN (Scottish and Southern Electricity Networks) Community Resilience Fund for a portable generator to support the community during power outages. The Board discussed the replacement of electric vehicle charge points as an action item during the year. The South Nesting Hall is being prepared as a resilience hub for the community during emergencies.

### Housing Initiatives

The Board investigated the Rural Islands Housing Fund as a potential source of support for addressing local housing needs, though this application was not pursued further. The Community Action Plan identified affordable housing as a priority, and CDCN continues to explore options for addressing this need in partnership with relevant agencies.

### Community Heritage Trail

A funding application for the South Nesting Community Heritage Trail was submitted to Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE), requesting £15,000. The project aims to create an engaging heritage trail showcasing South Nesting's history, with signage, lighting, and access pathways at sites including Gletness and Ling Ness. The application was submitted in March 2026, with approval anticipated. Mark Ratter was responsible for completing the grant submission.

### South Nesting Public Hall

CDCN continued to support South Nesting Public Hall, with rental and maintenance activities recorded during the period. The hall serves as a venue for community events and services, including the Nesting Gym. An electrical switchboard upgrade at the hall was funded during the year. Parking issues affecting the hall were noted as supporting the case for halls improvements and expansion.

### Development Officer Post

The Development Officer post, funded by Highlands and Islands Enterprise, continued to support CDCN's activities. A meeting was held on 6 March 2026 with Fiona Stirling of HIE regarding grant funding for the Development Officer position for a further 12 months (April 2026 to March 2027). The final allocation of £6,175 from HIE was confirmed before March 2026. Discussions took place regarding retaining Mark Ratter on a consultancy basis under the new DO funding arrangements.

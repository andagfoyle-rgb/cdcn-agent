# Trustee Report — 2025/2026

## Objectives and Activities

### The Aald Skül

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) was founded in December 2016 by local residents seeking to save the former South Nesting Primary School, known as the Aald Skül, from disposal by Shetland Islands Council. The group successfully campaigned to retain the building for community benefit, and CDCN was incorporated as a company limited by guarantee on 22 February 2018 (Company Number 589570). The organisation achieved charitable status on 27 February 2018, registered with the Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator (OSCR) under charity number SC048164.

The Aald Skül remains CDCN's primary asset and forms the hub of its community development activities. The building provides space for a range of services and projects that support community wellbeing and local economic activity.

### Community Ideas

CDCN operates as a membership-based organisation, committed to community-led development that responds to local needs and priorities. The organisation conducted a community survey to inform its activities and continues to engage residents in shaping future projects. This survey work contributed to the development of the Community Action Plan 2024–2034, which sets out the long-term vision for South Nesting.

CDCN remains committed to supporting initiatives that emerge from within the community, working with local partners and volunteers to deliver services that benefit residents of all ages.

### Nesting Scrapstore

The Nesting Scrapstore continued to operate from its premises at the Aald Skül, providing salvage materials for community projects and creative reuse. The Scrapstore Facebook page maintained an active following, supporting engagement and promotion of activities.

### Energy Efficiency and Solar PV Installation

CDCN secured funding for solar panel and battery storage installation through CARES Scotland and the Shetland Community Benefit Fund. The project aimed to reduce energy costs and improve the environmental sustainability of the Aald Skül. Installation work progressed during the year, with associated electrical works including an upgraded switchboard at Nesting Hall.

The Board discussed options for applying for a feed-in tariff to generate revenue from excess energy production.

### South Nesting Public Hall

CDCN continued to support South Nesting Public Hall, with work progressing on improvements to the facility. Funding from the SSEN Community Resilience Fund supported preparations to designate the hall as a resilience hub, providing backup facilities during power cuts. A portable generator was acquired to support this function.

### Community Heritage Trail

CDCN developed plans for a South Nesting Community Heritage Trail, with funding applications submitted to Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE). The project aims to create signage, lighting, and access pathways connecting heritage sites across South Nesting, including locations at Gletness and Ling Ness.

# Board Proposal — Cardio Gym Portacabin Project

**Applicant Organisation:** Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN)
**Scottish Charity Number:** SC048164
**Date of Proposal:** 22 March 2026
**Estimated Project Cost:** £40,000 – £44,000
**Project Title:** Dedicated Cardio Gym Facility at Aald Skül

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This proposal seeks Board approval to develop a dedicated cardio gym facility at the Aald Skül site in South Nesting. A survey of the top 30 gym users conducted in December 2025 showed strong preference for a separate cardio room over other expansion options. The project involves purchasing and installing a **new** portacabin (£30,000), fitting it out with three or four pieces of commercial-grade cardio equipment (£8,000–£12,000), and completing flooring and interior works (£1,500–£2,000). The total estimated cost is **£40,000–£44,000**, with potential funding sources including Highlands and Islands Enterprise (HIE), SUEZ, the Coastal Communities Fund, Shetland Community Benefit Fund, and CLLD/LEADER programmes. A meeting with HIE took place in early March 2026 to discuss funding options.

---

## 2. Background and Rationale

### Current Position

The Nesting Gym at Aald Skül currently offers free weights facilities but has **no dedicated cardio space**. Gym usage has grown steadily, and members have expressed interest in expanding the equipment offering to include cardiovascular training options.

### User Consultation

In December 2025, the gym management group (led by Alan Lindsay) surveyed the top 30 gym users, presenting three options:

1. Maintain the gym at its current size with no changes
2. Expand to a larger gym space, dropping exclusive-use booking arrangements
3. Create a two-room setup with one room dedicated to cardio equipment

**Option 3 received the strongest support**, indicating clear community demand for a separate cardio facility.

### Precedent and Learning

The Scrapstore previously secured a portacabin through ASEP funding (£112,117 from the Islands Programme 75% plus HIE 25%), which was delivered in April 2023. While that project provides a useful precedent for portacabin procurement and installation, **this proposal is for a new portacabin specifically for gym use — not a reuse of the existing Scrapstore building**.

An assault bike was ordered for the gym in December 2025, demonstrating ongoing investment in equipment. At the December 2025 meeting, Ellis Keith estimated that a full new gym building could cost £300,000–£350,000, with SUEZ identified as a potential funder for larger-scale development. This portacabin proposal represents a more immediate and cost-effective step towards meeting current user demand.

## 3. Proposed Solution

### Facility Specification

CDCN proposes to purchase and install a **new portacabin** on the Aald Skül grounds, positioned near the existing gym. The building would provide a dedicated space for cardio training, separating this activity from the free weights area.

**Proposed equipment (three or four pieces total):**
- One or two treadmills
- Two or three additional cardio machines (exact models to be determined by user survey)

A follow-up survey of the most active gym members will determine the final equipment mix before purchase.

### Cost Breakdown

| Item | Description | Estimated Cost |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| New portacabin | Purchase, delivery, installation, and basic electrics | £30,000 |
| Cardio equipment | Three or four quality commercial machines | £8,000 – £12,000 |
| Flooring and fit-out | Rubber flooring, finishing, and minor internal works | £1,500 – £2,000 |
| **Total estimated project cost** | | **£40,000 – £44,000** |

> **Note on running costs:** Ongoing electricity and heating costs will arise from operating the portacabin. Storage heaters were discussed at the December 2025 meeting as a suitable option for this type of building. These revenue costs will be factored into operational budgeting once capital funding is secured.

## 4. Site and Planning Considerations

The precise location on the Aald Skül grounds is **to be confirmed**. The likely position is adjacent to the existing gym building to minimise cabling runs and allow convenient access for users.

**Planning permission may need checking** with Shetland Islands Council (SIC), depending on the exact siting, size, and any conditions attached to the Aald Skül site use. This will be investigated as part of the next steps below.

## 1. What is CDCN Agent?

CDCN Agent is an artificial intelligence assistant designed to support the operational and administrative work of Community Development Company Nesting. It is a digital tool that can understand natural language requests, search through organisational records, draft documents, and help manage information — all through simple conversation.

Think of it as a knowledgeable assistant that has access to CDCN's documents, policies, meeting minutes, and funding records, and can help you find information or prepare written materials quickly.

---

## 2. Why was CDCN Agent created?

CDCN Agent was created to address several practical challenges faced by the Board and staff:

- **Reducing administrative burden** — Volunteer directors and part-time staff often spend significant time on routine administrative tasks such as drafting documents, searching for information, and preparing reports. CDCN Agent can handle much of this groundwork.

- **Improving access to information** — Important organisational knowledge is often scattered across multiple files, folders, and systems. CDCN Agent can search and retrieve relevant information in seconds.

- **Supporting funding activities** — Securing and managing grants requires consistent documentation, monitoring, and reporting. CDCN Agent can help track deadlines, draft applications, and maintain records.

- **Preserving institutional memory** — With changes in personnel and the passage of time, organisational knowledge can be lost. CDCN Agent maintains a structured memory of key information.

---

## 3. What can CDCN Agent do?

CDCN Agent has four main capabilities:

**3.1 Search the Archive**

You can ask CDCN Agent to find information from CDCN's records. Examples include:
- "Find all mentions of the Heritage Trail project in our minutes"
- "What did we decide about the solar panel installation?"
- "Show me our current funding applications"

**3.2 Draft Documents**

CDCN Agent can prepare drafts of common document types:
- Meeting agendas and minutes
- Funding applications and reports
- Policy documents
- Briefing notes and correspondence

All drafts are clearly marked as requiring review and approval before use.

**3.3 Check Funding Status**

CDCN Agent maintains a record of funding applications and can:
- Report on the status of current applications
- Track upcoming deadlines
- Identify reporting requirements for active grants

**3.4 Manage Memory**

CDCN Agent stores key organisational information:
- Funder details and preferences
- Project status and progress
- Important dates and deadlines
- Contact information for partners and stakeholders

## 6. What CDCN Agent Cannot Do

It is important to understand the limitations of CDCN Agent:

- **It does not make decisions** — CDCN Agent provides information and drafts, but all decisions remain with the Board, staff, and authorised individuals.

- **It does not replace human judgment** — Documents drafted by CDCN Agent must be reviewed and approved by appropriate persons before use. The agent may not fully capture nuanced context or recent developments.

- **It does not have access to everything** — CDCN Agent can only access information that has been provided to it. Documents stored only in paper form or in systems not connected to the agent will not be searchable.

- **It cannot guarantee accuracy** — While CDCN Agent strives for accuracy, all information should be verified against original sources where important decisions depend on it.

- **It does not share sensitive information inappropriately** — CDCN Agent is designed with data governance in mind, but users should still exercise caution with confidential matters.

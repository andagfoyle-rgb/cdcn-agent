## 6. Recommendations

1. **Systematic action tracking** — The Policy documents propose a digital tracking system (Kanban board) for funding applications and actions. Implementation would address the pattern of actions being assigned without systematic follow-up.

2. **Regular review agenda item** — Consider adding a standing item to board agendas for "Outstanding Actions Review" to ensure items are either progressed or formally closed.

3. **Clear completion recording** — Minutes should explicitly record when actions are completed, rather than simply dropping items from subsequent agendas.

4. **Prioritisation exercise** — The board may wish to formally review the list of outstanding items and agree which remain active priorities.

## 7. Methodology Note

This review was prepared based on document relationship analysis and available excerpts from CDCN minute files. A comprehensive review would require systematic examination of each minute file to extract:

- Every **ACTION:** entry
- Every **RESOLVED:** decision
- Follow-up status in subsequent meetings
- Completion evidence

**Recommendation:** The board should consider conducting a full systematic review or engaging administrative support to maintain a live action tracker.

## 1. Organisation Overview

Community Development Company Nesting (CDCN) is a Scottish charitable organisation based in South Nesting, Shetland. We serve the local community by providing various services and initiatives that aim to improve the quality of life for our residents. Our organisation has a track record of delivering successful projects that have positively impacted the community.

Some of our notable achievements include:

* Developing and managing Polycrub Rentals, a sustainable gardening initiative that provides affordable plots for residents.
* Organising community events and activities that promote social cohesion and well-being.
* Collaborating with local partners to enhance community facilities and services.

We are committed to working closely with the community to identify their needs and priorities. Our projects are designed to be inclusive, accessible, and beneficial to all members of the community.

## 2. Project Description

The South Nesting Community Heritage Trail is a project that aims to create a unique and engaging experience for visitors and residents alike. The trail will feature a series of interpretive panels, artworks, and historical markers that showcase the rich history and culture of our area.

The project will involve:

* Conducting historical research and consultation with local experts to develop the content and design of the trail.
* Working with local artists and craftspeople to create interactive and engaging exhibits.
* Installing the trail infrastructure, including signage, lighting, and access pathways.

The project will be delivered in partnership with local stakeholders, including Shetland Islands Council, Highlands and Islands Enterprise, and community groups. Our team will ensure that the project is managed efficiently and effectively, with regular progress updates and stakeholder engagement throughout the delivery phase.

## 3. Intended Outcomes

The following are our intended outcomes for this project:

* The creation of a unique and engaging heritage trail that showcases the rich history and culture of South Nesting.
* Increased visitor numbers to the area, with a focus on promoting local tourism and economic development.
* Improved community engagement and social cohesion through the creation of a shared sense of place and identity.

We will measure the success of this project through:

* Visitor numbers and feedback surveys.
* Community participation and engagement metrics.
* Economic impact analysis and return-on-investment (ROI) assessments.
